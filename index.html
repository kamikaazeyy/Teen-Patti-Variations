Diwali Table Pro-Tip

Standard Teen Patti Hand Rankings

From highest to lowest (except in Muflis where this exact order is reversed!):

#1 HIGHEST Trail / Trio / Set

Three cards of the same rank. Three Aces (A-A-A) is the highest; three 2s (2-2-2) is the lowest trail.

A♠ A♥ A♦
#2 Pure Sequence (Pakki Rung)

Three consecutive cards of the exact same suit. (A-K-Q is highest, followed by A-2-3).

A♥ K♥ Q♥
#3 Sequence (Run)

Three consecutive cards of mixed suits.

10♠ 9♦ 8♣
#4 Color (Flush)

Any three cards of the same suit, not in sequence.

K♦ J♦ 4♦
#5 Pair (Joot)

Two cards of the same rank + one kicker card.

Q♠ Q♣ 7♥
#6 LOWEST High Card

Three unmatched cards. Decided by highest individual card (Ace high, 2 low).

A♠ 9♦ 4♣
